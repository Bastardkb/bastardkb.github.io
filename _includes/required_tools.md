# Required Tools

Before starting the build, check that you have all the required tools:

- Soldering iron
- Soldering tin with rosin core
- TX10 (M3) Torx screw driver (a flat head will also work)*
- TX20 (M4) Torx screw driver (a flat head will also work)
- Cutting pliers
- Tweezers (normally closed recommended)

*A TX10 screwdriver is not required for the Scylla, TBK Mini, Skeletyl.

We also recommend the following tools:

- multimeter
- desoldering pump and/or solder wick (mistakes happen)
- spare flux

Also make sure to check the contents of the package carefully to make sure that everything is there.

# Before you build: safety information

Our DIY kits need soldering. Read this whole page before you start. If anything is unclear, stop and email support@bastardkb.com.

## Who can build this kit

**Assembly is for adults aged 18 or older only.** Do not let children solder or handle the tools. Keep the kit, tools and loose parts away from children and pets.

## Set up your workspace

- Work on a stable, heat-resistant surface, in a well-ventilated room with good lighting
- Keep flammable items (paper, cloth, liquids) away from your work area
- Always rest the iron in its stand. Never leave it on and unattended
- Switch the iron off and let it cool fully before putting it away
- Tie back long hair and avoid loose sleeves

## Protect yourself

- Wear safety glasses. Solder can splash and cut leads can fly off
- Cut component leads with flush cutters, and hold the lead or cover it so the clipped end doesn't fly off
- Never touch the iron tip or freshly soldered joints. They stay hot for longer than you expect
- The PCB edges and trimmed leads can be sharp. Handle them carefully

## Solder

- Use lead-free solder (RoHS compliant).
- Lead-free solder might need a higher iron temperature than leaded solder, so take extra care with burns
- Don't breathe the fumes. Use a fume extractor or work near an open window
- Don't eat, drink or smoke while soldering, and wash your hands when you finish

## Small parts

Switches, keycaps, the trackball, screws and cut leads are small and can be a choking hazard. Keep them away from children and pets, and collect all trimmed leads when you're done.

## Before you plug it in

1. Inspect every joint for solder bridges, loose strands or stray bits of solder
2. Use a multimeter to confirm there is no short between 5V and GND
3. Connect the keyboard directly to your computer's USB port. Don't plug in a board that looks damaged, smells hot or has any exposed solder bridges
4. If anything gets unusually hot, smells, smokes or sparks, unplug it immediately and contact us
